Questions

Corporate travel, answered

What does a corporate travel agent do?

A corporate travel agent books and manages business travel for your company: flights, hotels, transfers, visas and trip changes, with one point of contact, negotiated rates and itemised invoicing.

What group sizes do you handle?

From a single business traveller to groups of 100+. Our largest coordinated event had more than 100 attendees, including group flights, hotel blocks and transfers.

How fast will I get a quote?

A consultant calls you back and sends a written quote the same day on working days. Office hours are Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:30pm and Sat 8:30am–1pm.

Do you offer team building and corporate retreat packages in Kenya?

Yes. We organise team building and corporate retreats at safari lodges, bush resorts and coastal hotels, including transport, meals and activities. See the packages above for starting prices.

Can you invoice our company?

Yes. We prepare itemised corporate invoices. If your team travels often, ask about an ongoing business travel arrangement during the call.

Do you handle conferences and international delegations?

Yes, inbound and outbound: group flights, visa assistance, hotel blocks, ground transfers and arrival logistics for conference delegates.

Can you also arrange car hire, transfers and travel insurance?

Yes. We add airport transfers, car hire and travel insurance to the same quote, so everything is booked and invoiced together.

Do you work with NGOs and event organisers?

Yes. We support NGOs, project teams and event organisers with field travel, workshops, delegate travel and group bookings.

Can you customise a package to our budget?

Yes. The packages above are starting points. We adjust destination, nights, meals, activities and meeting space to suit your team and budget.

Are the "From" prices final?

They are indicative per-person-sharing rates. Final pricing depends on season, group size and availability, and is confirmed in your written quote.
