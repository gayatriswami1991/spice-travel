Questions

Travel agent questions, answered

What does a travel agent in Nairobi do?

We book your flights, hotels, transfers, safaris and holidays, help with visa applications and handle changes, so you deal with one team instead of many.

Are you an IATA-certified travel agency?

Yes. Spice Travel is IATA certified, which lets us issue international airline tickets and meet global travel industry standards.

Can you book flights on any airline?

Yes. We book domestic and international flights through Amadeus and Travelport systems, including group and last-minute bookings.

Do you organise safaris and holiday packages?

Yes. We offer Kenya safari packages, beach holidays and international trips. See the packages above for starting prices.

Do you handle corporate and group travel?

Yes. We manage business travel, conferences and team-building retreats for groups from 5 to 100+ travellers, with itemised invoices.

Do you help with visa applications?

Yes. We guide the application and documents for destinations such as Dubai, Europe and the UK.

How fast will I get a quote?

A consultant calls you back and sends a written quote the same day on working days. Office hours are Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:30pm and Sat 8:30am–1pm.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes. We provide travel insurance options that cover medical emergencies, trip delays and cancellations.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. They are indicative starting prices. Final pricing depends on season, group size and availability, and is confirmed in your written quote.
