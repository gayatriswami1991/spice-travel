Questions

Holiday packages, answered

What does "per person sharing" mean?

The price is per person when two people share a room. A single traveller may pay a supplement. Your quote shows the exact price for your group.

What does "self-drive" mean in a package?

You drive your own vehicle to the lodge. The package covers the accommodation and meals listed. We can also quote flights or transfers if you prefer not to drive.

Are park and conservancy fees included?

It depends on the package. For example, Loisaba and Elsa's Kopje exclude conservancy fees, and Soroi Amboseli excludes park fees, transport and game drives. Your quote lists what is included and what is extra.

Can you include flights in my holiday?

Yes. Some packages include return tickets, and we can add flights to any other package. Tell us your dates and we will quote it together.

Do you help with visas for trips abroad?

Yes. We guide the application and documents for destinations such as Dubai, Europe and the UK. See our visa assistance page.

How fast will I get my quote?

A consultant calls you back and sends a written quote the same day on working days. Office hours are Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:30pm and Sat 8:30am–1pm.

Can I change a package to suit my budget or dates?

Yes. Packages are starting points. We adjust nights, room types, meals, activities and dates to fit you.

Can you plan a family or group holiday?

Yes. We plan holidays for families, friends and groups, with shared transport and group rates where available.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes. We provide travel insurance options that cover medical emergencies, trip delays and cancellations.

Are the prices final?

No. They are indicative starting prices per person sharing. Final pricing depends on season, group size and availability, and is confirmed in your written quote.
