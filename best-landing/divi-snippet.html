Questions

Corporate travel and travel agent questions, answered

What does a corporate travel agent do?

A corporate travel agent books and manages business travel for your company: flights, hotels, transfers, visas and trip changes, with one point of contact, negotiated rates and itemised invoicing.

Why choose Spice Travel as your travel agent in Nairobi?

We are IATA certified, have served Nairobi since 1994, are rated 5 on Google and have managed groups of more than 100 travellers. One team handles flights, hotels, transfers, visas and insurance.

What group sizes do you handle?

From a single business traveller to groups of 100+. Our largest coordinated event had more than 100 attendees, including group flights, hotel blocks and transfers.

Do you offer team building and corporate retreat packages in Kenya?

Yes. We organise team building and corporate retreats at safari lodges, bush resorts and coastal hotels, including transport, meals and activities. See the retreat packages above for starting prices.

Can you book flights on any airline?

Yes. We book domestic and international flights through Amadeus and Travelport systems, including group and last-minute bookings.

How fast will I get a quote?

A consultant calls you back and sends a written quote the same day on working days. Office hours are Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:30pm and Sat 8:30am–1pm.

Can you invoice our company?

Yes. We prepare itemised corporate invoices. If your team travels often, ask about an ongoing business travel arrangement during the call.

Do you handle conferences, NGOs and international delegations?

Yes, inbound and outbound: group flights, visa assistance, hotel blocks, ground transfers and arrival logistics for conference delegates and project teams.

Do you also sell holiday and safari packages?

Yes. We offer Kenya safari packages, beach holidays and international trips for individuals, families and groups. Prices on this page are indicative starting prices per person sharing.

Can you arrange car hire, transfers and travel insurance?

Yes. We add airport transfers, car hire and travel insurance to the same quote, so everything is booked and invoiced together.
