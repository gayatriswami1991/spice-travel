# Optional photo for the page (prompt for any AI image tool)

The page uses only real photos (your Instagram photos and the images from your zip). There are no cartoons or illustrations.

If you want a real-looking team photo in the corporate section, create it, name it exactly `team-meeting-outdoors.webp`
(WebP, about 1600 x 900 px, under 200 KB), upload it to the Media Library next to the other images and use the light
file `divi-snippet.html`. It then covers the lodge photo in that spot automatically.

| File name | Where it appears | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `team-meeting-outdoors.webp` | Corporate section | Photorealistic, a diverse group of eight business colleagues in smart-casual clothes holding a strategy meeting outdoors under a large acacia tree at a Kenyan safari lodge, long wooden table with laptops and notebooks, golden hour light, relaxed and smiling, shallow depth of field, 16:9, no text, no logos |

Tips: check faces and hands before using. Do not present AI images as photos of a specific hotel.
