# Images to create for the page (prompts for any AI image tool)

I cannot generate photos here. The page already works without them: it uses your real Instagram photos plus two
drawn illustrations (a team meeting under an acacia tree, and an aeroplane above the clouds).

If you create photos, name them exactly as below, save as WebP (about 1600 x 900 px, under 200 KB), upload them to the
Media Library next to the other images and use `divi-snippet.html` (the light file). They replace the illustrations by themselves.
(`divi-snippet-with-images.html` has everything embedded, so it cannot pick up new files.)
